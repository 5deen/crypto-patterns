Accounts

Sign in

There is nothing to sign in to yet — and nothing on this site currently requires it.

Accounts are not open

crypto-patterns has no login. The site is static — there is no server to hold an account, no session, and no password to store. Rather than put up a form that pretends otherwise, this page says so plainly.

Beta access is handled as a list. Add yourself to it and you will be contacted directly when there is something to sign in to.

Join the beta list

The demo needs no account

The pattern demo on the home page is open to everyone. It runs entirely in your browser, which is also why putting a login in front of it would not mean much: the code that draws the pattern is already downloaded to your machine. Gating it would hide a button, not protect anything.

When accounts do arrive

Real sign-in needs somewhere for credentials to live that is not the visitor’s browser. When that exists, this page becomes the login and the privacy policy will be updated in the same change to say what is stored and why.
