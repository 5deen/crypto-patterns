What happens to what you enter

Airtable, Inc. receives and stores your submission — it is the processor for the beta list, and it is based in the United States, so signing up involves a transfer to a third country. Section 6 of the privacy policy sets out the detail: what is collected, the legal basis, how long it is kept, and how to have it deleted.

You can ask for your entry to be removed at any time, without giving a reason, by writing to the address on the contact page.

You do not need an account to try it

The pattern demo on the home page is open to everyone and always will be. It runs entirely in your browser, so gating it behind a login would be theatre — the code is already on your machine either way. Signing up gets you early access to what comes next, not access to the demo.
